# Navigation & intuitiveness review — 2026-09-17

How the app feels to move through, measured in taps from where she is to
what she wants, walked on a touch-emulated 390×844 phone with the e2e
harness and by reading every screen. The first section is what changed
today; the rest is what should change next, ranked.

## Fixed today

### 1. The keyboard no longer ambushes her

**Before.** Sixteen surfaces auto-focused their first field on open —
every create sheet, the auth screen, onboarding, the quick note, the
feedback sheet. On a phone that means: tap +, the sheet slides up, the
keyboard slides up over it, the form she has not read yet is half
covered, and the button she was reaching for has moved. It felt jerky
because two animations fought for the same 300 ms.

**Now.** `lib/device.ts::prefersAutoFocus()` returns false on any
touch device (`pointer: coarse`), and every `autoFocus` and every
programmatic focus-on-open goes through it. She taps the field she
wants; the keyboard appears then. On a laptop the courtesy stays.

The two deliberate exceptions are the search boxes (global search,
find-in-note): typing is their only purpose, and a search box that
makes you tap it first is a worse search box. The e2e harness now runs
with `hasTouch` and asserts, for every sheet it opens, that nothing has
focus on open — so a future sheet that regresses this fails CI.

### 2. One + for the whole app, and it fans out

**Before.** Hoy had a speed-dial; every other screen had a plain + that
opened one sheet. Creating a piece from Hoy was menu → Obra → + (three
taps and a screen change); creating a sale from Obra was menu → Dinero
→ +. The + meant a different thing on every screen and gave no hint
which.

**Now.** `QuickAddFab` is mounted once in `App.tsx`, on every screen but
Ajustes (which has nothing to add). A tap rotates it 45° and fans out
the options — Cardigan's pattern — with the **current screen's own
action nearest her thumb** and the quick actions above it. Agenda's
entry carries the selected day; Dinero's flips between Nueva venta and
Nuevo gasto with the segmented control; Notas hides the button while
she is multi-selecting. Screens register that one action with
`useFab()`; the generic sheets live inside the FAB, so no screen renders
a `.fab` any more. "New anything" is two taps from anywhere.

### 3. The back chevron takes her back

**Before.** `back()` went to a fixed parent: Obra → Contactos → chevron
landed on **Hoy**, not Obra. Every drawer-to-drawer hop lost her place.

**Now.** The chevron returns to the tab she came from, and only falls
back to the parent when she arrived from another drawer route (Obra →
Recurrentes → chevron is Dinero, which is right). The OS back gesture is
untouched: it walks the hash history as before.

### 4. Dinero reaches its own sub-screens

Recurrentes, Presupuestos, Pronóstico and Reportes were drawer-only —
two taps and a menu away from the screen they belong to. Dinero now
ends with a chip row that opens each in one tap.

## Tap distance, before → after

Counted from Hoy unless noted; "menu" is the ☰ drawer.

| Job | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| New sale / expense / event / piece / contact / tarea / note | 2 (+ → option) | 2 |
| New piece **from Agenda or Dinero** | 3 + screen change (menu → Obra → +) | 2 (+ → Nueva pieza) |
| New event on a specific day | Agenda → day → + | same, and the FAB still carries the day |
| New expense from Dinero → Gastos | 1 (the + was context-aware) | 2 (+ → Nuevo gasto, nearest) |
| Register a payment on a sale | Dinero → sale → Registrar pago = 3 | 3 — see P1 below |
| Reportes from Dinero | menu → Reportes = 2 | 1 (chip) |
| Back from Contactos to Obra | 2 (chevron → Hoy → menu → Obra = 3) | 1 |
| Tell Diego something | — | 2 (menu → Cuéntale a Diego) |

The one regression is deliberate: on Dinero → Gastos the + used to open
the expense sheet directly (one tap); it is now two, because a + that
does something different on every screen is the thing that made it feel
unpredictable. The gasto is the nearest option, so the second tap is
a 4 mm move.

## What should change next, ranked

**P1 — Registering a payment is the most frequent money action and the
deepest.** She gets paid more often than she makes a sale, and it takes
Dinero → the sale → Registrar pago, with the sale hidden in a list once
she has more than a screen of them. Recommendation: a quick action
**"Registrar pago"** that opens a picker of open sales (Por cobrar,
soonest due first) and then `PaymentSheet` with the sale pre-selected.
Two taps from anywhere. Small: `PaymentSheet` and the balance math
already exist; this is one picker.

**P1 — The speed-dial is seven items and she cannot trim it.**
`settings.quickActions` controls the order, but nothing in Ajustes
edits it, so every user gets all seven. Seven is scannable on an 844 px
phone (verified: the top item clears the topbar) but it is a lot of
reading for "I want to add an expense". Recommendation: an **"Acciones
rápidas"** editor in Ajustes → Perfil (ChipMultiSelect + drag or
up/down), and a default of five (sale, expense, event, piece, note)
for new workspaces. Half a day.

**P2 — Obra and Contactos live in the drawer.** For a working artist
these are daily, and the pill only has three slots — a deliberate
thumb-reach choice from the design system. The FAB now removes the
*creation* reason to visit them, which is most of it. Recommendation:
**decide from data, not taste.** Diagnóstico already counts route
visits; after two weeks of the pilot, if Obra or Contactos outrank
Agenda, swap it into the pill. Do not add a fourth slot: the pill's
labels already sit at the floor of legibility at 390 px.

**P2 — Hoy is a dashboard, not a launcher.** It does the attention list
and the numbers well, but reaching a *thing* (a piece she is working
on, the client she is meeting) means the drawer. Recommendation: a
compact **"Seguir con…"** row on Hoy — the last two pieces edited, the
next contact with a follow-up — as tappable rows opening their sheets.
The data is all in context already. One afternoon.

**P2 — Search is one tap away and nobody knows.** The magnifier in the
topbar reaches everything she has written, but it looks like a
decoration next to the avatar. Recommendation: on the drawer screens
with a list (Obra, Contactos, Notas, Estudios), the local `SearchField`
already exists — good. On Hoy, a placeholder-styled search row under
the greeting ("Buscar una pieza, un cliente, una nota…") that opens the
global sheet would teach the icon. Cheap, but measure first: the route
tally will show whether search is used at all.

**P3 — Sheet stacking on the money detail.** Dinero → sale → Registrar
pago stacks a sheet on a sheet; dismissing the top one lands on the
detail, which is right, but the handle-drag on the top sheet can catch
the one beneath on a fast flick. Recommendation: the second sheet
should mount with the first one's scrim frozen (no drag) — a
`Sheet` prop, not a new component.

**P3 — Ajustes doubles as a directory.** "Tus datos" lists Recurrentes,
Presupuestos and Archivos beside export and diagnostics. With the Dinero
chips those two rows are now reachable where they belong; leave them
(a second door hurts nobody) but stop adding navigation rows to
Ajustes.

**P3 — The drawer's "Angus" group now has three items** (Ajustes, Tu
cuenta, Cuéntale a Diego). Fine at three. If a fourth appears, "Tu
cuenta" should fold into Ajustes — the avatar already opens it.

## Not changed on purpose

- **Three tabs.** Thumb-reach and label legibility on 360–390 px are
  real limits; the FAB and the back fix remove most of the pressure.
- **Sheets, not routes, for detail.** The OS back gesture walking the
  hash history is a known, documented trade (`hooks/useEscape.ts`).
- **The onboarding length.** Eight steps, each skippable, each
  persisted as answered. It is long, but every answer changes Hoy.

## How this was measured

`scripts/e2e-smoke.mjs` on a touch-emulated 390×844 Chromium against a
local build, signed in as `angus-e2e@cardigan.mx`: every tab, every
drawer route, every create sheet through the FAB, the create → edit →
delete → reload journey, with a focus assertion on every sheet open.
Screenshots of the menus and Dinero were reviewed by eye. Tap counts
are from reading the screens' handlers, not from the harness.
